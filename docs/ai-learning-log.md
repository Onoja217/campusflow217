# CampusFlow AI-assisted learning log

This log records AI-assisted learning for the CampusFlow assignment. It is evidence of learning, not a list of AI-generated answers. Each fellow must complete entries from their own real interactions, experiments, and verification. Do not copy an interaction you did not personally have, invent results, or claim a test ran unless you observed its output.

## How to complete an entry

For each interaction, record the actual prompt or question, what you believed before asking, what you tried, what actually happened, how you verified it, and whether you accepted, improved, or rejected the suggestion. Link the relevant commit, PR, test output, or code where possible. At least three genuine interactions per fellow are required, including at least one suggestion corrected, improved, or rejected.
